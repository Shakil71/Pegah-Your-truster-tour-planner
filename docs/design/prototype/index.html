Field notes

Ratargul is a forest you visit by boat.

It is one of the few freshwater swamp forests left in Bangladesh. From June to September the water rises around the trunks and the only way through is a hand-paddled dinghy.

Go early. The light comes through the canopy before ten, and the forest is quiet.
